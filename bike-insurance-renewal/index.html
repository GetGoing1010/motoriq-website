Two-wheeler renewal enquiries

Your contact details are submitted voluntarily so the MotorIQ motor team can follow up about your stated insurance requirement. Submitting an enquiry does not itself create a purchase obligation.

What to keep ready

Vehicle make/model, registration number if available, vehicle type, current insurer and policy-expiry information where applicable. Accurate contact details help the team respond to your enquiry.

How MotorIQ works

1. Submit your requirement. 2. MotorIQ receives the enquiry. 3. The motor team reviews it and contacts you using the details you provided.

MotorIQ is a lead-capture and routing platform. Genuine customer enquiries are created when customers voluntarily submit their details or through an attributable partner request. MotorIQ does not create individual customer enquiries from public vehicle or government datasets.
