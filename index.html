Frequently asked questions

Can I request a fresh policy?
Yes. Select the new-policy requirement in the MotorIQ quote form.

Can I request a renewal?
Yes. Provide your current insurer and policy-expiry information where available.

Do I have to purchase a policy after submitting?
No. Submitting an enquiry does not itself create a purchase obligation.

What information should I keep ready?
Vehicle make/model, vehicle type, registration number if available, current insurer and policy-expiry date where applicable.
